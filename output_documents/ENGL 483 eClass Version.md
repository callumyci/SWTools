## Guided Listening Practice Sign-Up Sheet

One of your assignments is a Guided Listening Practice , explained in
more detail in our syllabus. Most of our Fridays will be devoted to this
assignment, and there will be a maximum of three presentations on those
days. You're required to choose one day on which you will lead a guided
listening practice. Please consider what date what might be best for you
and enter your name on our sign-up sheet here.

A couple of notes: 1) I'd strongly encourage you to choose a date early
in the term when you're not as busy; 2) once you've committed to a date,
it may be hard to change that, or the alternative might not be to your
liking, so please give this careful thought and make the best choice
possible.

presentation, please upload it to this google docs folder so you can
access it from the podium for playback. You can upload a sound file or
you can upload a Word doc with a weblink depending on your source.
